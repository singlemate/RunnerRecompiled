README	Mirror's Edge for Android, prepared from your copy of the game\n\nIn this folder:\n  RunnerRecompiled-pc-personal.apk  the app\n  MirrorsEdge-GOG.zip               your game files\n\nWhat to do:\n1. Copy the APK and MirrorsEdge-GOG.zip to your tablet, for example to Download.\n2. Install the APK. Android will ask you to allow installing from this source.\n3. Open the app and choose MirrorsEdge-GOG.zip when it asks for the game archive.\n4. If the game does not start or the picture has artifacts, open Graphics driver in the app's settings and choose a ZIP driver package for AdrenoTools (for example, Turnip).\n\nYou need an Android device with Vulkan and an ARMv8.4 or newer CPU.\n\nThe APK and the archive contain data from your licensed copy of the game. Keep them to yourself: do not publish or share them.\n
